# DI and power V0.5.1 — incomplete

The previous review was incomplete. A six-channel numerical scan now covers 0/12/20/24/28 V and assumed 1%/5% resistor tolerance; these voltages are not approved ratings. VF=0 is used for conservative resistor current and power bounds because a guaranteed minimum was not found. LED worst-case power and resistor derating remain open.

The six optocoupler outputs have 10k pull-ups to 3.3 V and feed MCU PE7–PE12. Shared input and output ground means the interface is not isolated. External sources must not be connected in parallel to the 12V pins before reverse-feed protection is designed and verified. The 81 supply scenarios are assumptions, exclude unknown loads and do not close the fuse or thermal design. No physical tests or manufacturing release.
