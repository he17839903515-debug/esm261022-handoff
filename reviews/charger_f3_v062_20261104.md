# Charger and F3 V0.6.2

U24 CN3703 has 12.6V nominal three-cell charge regulation; the existing 0.200 ohm sense resistor corresponds to a conditional 0.925–1.075A sense-current scenario. Actual battery and BMS remain unknown. U24 VCC is downstream of F3 and Q12, so charger input must be included in the source budget.

At hypothetical 12.6V battery and 1A charge, output power is 12.6W; at 80% conversion and 24V input, charger input is approximately 0.656A before upstream losses or other loads. A 0.1A Fuzetec comparison fuse is therefore incompatible as a hold-current design point; installed identity and trip timing remain unverified. CN3703 maximum operating input is 28V, so nominal 28V leaves no positive tolerance margin. All battery and charging functions remain in scope; no physical tests or signoff.
