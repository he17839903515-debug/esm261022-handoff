# Primary and battery power V0.6 — preliminary

PST-derived connectivity confirms the primary 13V source and the battery node feed the 12V rail through separate diodes. Installed diode manufacturers and ordering codes remain unknown. D14 is only identified as a generic TVS; clamping at the actual surge waveform cannot yet be established. A 33V TVS example with 53.3V clamp does not by itself protect 30V and 25V rated semiconductors.

U22 freewheel diode selection must account for maximum input transients and peak current. The same-name JIERR L5 comparison has 8.2uH, maximum 61 milliohm DCR, typical 5.5A saturation and 5A heating current; the schematic R0603 library field conflicts with its dimensions. The mode calculations use explicitly hypothetical diode parameters; reverse input, hot-plug, shorts and backfeed remain unresolved. No physical testing or manufacturing release.
