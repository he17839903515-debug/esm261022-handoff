# Main supply V0.3 — work in progress

Gate network analysis: with Q14 off and Q12 source near VIN, gate-source voltage can approach negative VIN. Q14 turn-on cannot be precisely predicted from a nominal 27 V zener value: at VIN=28 V and a hypothetical 27 V clamp, its 27k feed supplies only about 37 µA. The real low-current zener curve and transistor characteristics are unknown. A source-to-gate clamp is a candidate topology, not an approved design.

Fuzetec FSMD010-1206-R official series data identifies 0.1 A hold, 0.25 A trip, 60 V, and Rmin 1.6 ohm / R1max 15 ohm. The matching schematic library field does not establish installed identity. Four matching 0.36 W relays would require 1.44 W nominal before other loads; load and thermal protection coordination are incomplete. No measurements or production approval.
