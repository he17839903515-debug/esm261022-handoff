# J8 DI review V0.5 — incomplete

PST-derived connectivity shows six even input pins feeding individual 2.7k resistors and PS2501-1 LED anodes; all LED cathodes share board ground. Odd pins share the 12V bus. An external contact using the odd pin draws board current; an independently powered signal must be accounted for separately. Neither the actual input voltage range nor component tolerances and fitted identities are established.

With 2.7k and hypothetical 1.17V LED drop, a 12V input gives about 4.01mA and 43mW resistor dissipation, while a 24V example gives about 8.46mA and 193mW. These are illustrative conditions, not approved ratings. The load and fuse budget remains incomplete; no physical measurements or manufacturing release.
