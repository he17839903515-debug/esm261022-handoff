# Diode and inductor review V0.8

PST-derived D18 is the U22 freewheel diode; D19 and D24 connect the primary and battery sources to the 12V bus. Under illustrative ideal CCM conditions at VIN 28V, output 13.2V and 2A, 8.2uH and 1MHz, calculated D18 current is 1.057A average, 1.465A RMS and 2.425A peak; reverse voltage is approximately VIN before switching transients. Real fault and thermal conditions remain open.
