# Main supply review, preliminary

PST-derived connectivity confirms J10/F3/VIN/Q12/VOUT/U22/L5/13V/D19/12V and the D24 battery feed. Q12 control and actual F3 part ratings remain unverified. Q13 is recorded with source and drain on ground; do not infer a conventional switching function without further investigation. D14, D18, D19 and D24 are not uniquely identified by generic schematic attributes.

TI LMR14050 Rev B specifies 0.750 V typical feedback, 0.735–0.765 V over the stated junction-temperature range. 332k/20k gives 13.2 V nominal; 12.6944–13.7205 V is illustrative only with independent ±1% resistors and ignoring FB bias and thermal drift. Actual resistor tolerance and guaranteed bias are unknown. Battery voltage, load currents, diode forward drops and thermal conditions remain open. No tests or production release.
