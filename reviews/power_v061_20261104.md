# Power review V0.6.1 — conditional

PST-derived R66 is 23.7k from U22 RT to ground, corresponding to TI's typical 1 MHz setting. Ideal CCM calculations use 8.2 uH and its assumed ±20% inductance range; at 28 V input and 13.2 V output, typical 8.2 uH yields approximately 0.851 A peak-to-peak ripple and 2.425 A inductor peak at 2 A load. Frequency tolerance, transient spikes, fault conditions and actual loads remain open.

A conditional 12V rail of 12.1944–13.4205 V based on previous 13V sensitivity and assumed 0.3–0.5 V diode drop lies below the Songle 12 V coil table's 130% (15.6 V) maximum, but actual battery voltage, transients and installed relay are unknown. Battery chemistry and BMS voltage cannot be inferred from the controller or connector. Protection and device selections remain unsigned; no physical testing.
